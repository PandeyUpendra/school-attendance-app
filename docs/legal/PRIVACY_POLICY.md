## 1. Who we are and our role

This app is provided to schools as a service. **Each school is the data fiduciary
(controller)** of its students', guardians' and staff members' data: the school decides
what is collected and who may access it. **We act as the data processor**, storing and
processing that data on the school's instructions. Questions about a specific school's
data practices should be directed to that school; questions about the platform itself
come to us at the contact above.

## 2. What data the app processes

Accounts are created by the school — the app has no public self-registration.

| Category | Examples | Whose data |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Name, role (teacher/guardian/etc.), email address, phone number | Staff, guardians |
| Student records | Name, class, section, roll number, admission ID, guardian contact | Students (minors) |
| Attendance | Daily present/leave/absent status, reasons | Students, staff |
| Academic | Homework, exam marks, report cards, copy-check status, remarks | Students |
| Financial | Fee structures, fee payment records (amount, date, mode, receipt no.) | Guardians/students |
| Communication | Announcements, notifications, leave applications, meeting records | All roles |
| Photos | School gallery photos uploaded by the school; staff profile photos | Students, staff |
| Technical | Device push-notification token, crash reports, app-check attestation | All users |

We do **not** collect precise location, contacts, browsing history, or advertising
identifiers, and we do not show advertising.

## 3. Children's data and parental consent

Students are minors. Their data is entered and managed **by the school**, which is
responsible under the DPDP Act for obtaining **verifiable parental/guardian consent**
before their child's data is processed in the app. The app provides an in-app consent
record for guardians; a guardian may withdraw consent through the app or by contacting
the school, after which the school must remove the student's records. We do not use
children's data for advertising, profiling, tracking, or any purpose other than the
school services described here.

## 4. Why the data is processed (purposes and lawful basis)

Attendance keeping, academic records, fee records, school–guardian communication, staff
scheduling and substitution, and school administration — i.e. performance of the school's
educational functions, with consent obtained by the school where required. We process
nothing for marketing or resale.

## 5. Where the data lives and who processes it

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication, Firebase
Cloud Messaging, Firebase Storage, Crashlytics), operated by Google LLC. Servers are
located in [REGION — e.g. United States (us-central1) / Mumbai (asia-south1)]. Google
acts as a sub-processor under the Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum. Access to data
is restricted by role-based security rules: teachers see their classes, guardians see
only their own children, school management sees its own school. **No school can see
another school's data.**

## 6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data, ever. Data is shared only: (a) inside the school
per the role-based access above; (b) with Google as hosting sub-processor; (c) where
required by law or lawful order.

## 7. Retention

Data is retained while the school's contract is active and as the school directs.
When a student is removed, associated personal records (attendance entries, exam
results, fee links, guardian access) are deleted by an automated cascade. When a school
leaves the service, its entire data set is deleted on the school's instruction. Crash
logs are retained by Crashlytics for up to 90 days.

## 8. Security

Role-based Firestore security rules (default-deny), per-school tenant isolation,
Firebase App Check device attestation, TLS encryption in transit, encryption at rest by
Google, and audit logging of privileged actions. No system is perfectly secure; in the
event of a personal-data breach we will notify the affected schools and the Data
Protection Board of India as required by the DPDP Act.
